## Problem

Decisions and action items from operations meetings are lost or remembered differently by different people.

## Which JD requirement this proves

Collaborate cross-functionally to drive execution. A stretch goal, outside the JD's core five nouns.

## Who Uses It

Anyone running or attending an operations meeting.

## AI Usage

Transcribes audio via Whisper, then extracts decisions, action items, owners, deadlines, risks, and unresolved questions.
